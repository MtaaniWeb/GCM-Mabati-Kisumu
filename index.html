About GCM

Reliable roofing supply, backed by practical service.

GCM Mabati Factory Kisumu supplies roofing sheets, ridges and accessories for residential, commercial and other building projects. Based along Obunga Bypass in Kisumu, we help customers identify suitable roofing options and request pricing with confidence.

From profile selection to the details that complete a roof, our focus is on making the purchasing process straightforward. Wholesale and retail enquiries are welcome.
